How a question is shown

  1. The original stem — including the data-booklet table when the paper printed one.
  2. The original figure, stored as PNG / base64 from the exam crop, not a TikZ impersonation of the question.
  3. A B C D as buttons. Figure-only options (four plotted spectra) are letter buttons only.
